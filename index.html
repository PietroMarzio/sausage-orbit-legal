Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Doxie orbits a planet on its own. Tap anywhere to jump off, and fly into the next planet's orbit. Climb as high as you can before the vacuum catches up.

I bought something and I don't see it

Open Settings in the Game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Purchases are free to restore on all your iPhones.

How do lives work?

Levels use lives: one comes back every 20 minutes, up to 10. You can also watch a video to refill them all. Endless and the tutorial are always free.

What does the Pro Pup Pass do?

It removes the full-screen ads between runs, forever, and gives Doxie the golden coat. Rewarded videos stay available if you want their bonus.

How do I change my ad or privacy choices?

In the Game: Settings › Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it) and Settings › Share anonymous statistics. On the iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Everything is explained in the Privacy Policy.

I deleted the Game and lost my progress

Progress is saved only on your iPhone, so deleting the Game deletes it too. Purchases are not lost: use Restore purchases.

The Game has no sound

The Game follows the iPhone's silent switch: turn silent mode off, then check the volume.
