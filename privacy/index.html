Privacy Policy

Sausage Orbit: Space Dash · effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains what the iPhone game Sausage Orbit: Space Dash (the “Game”) does with data. The short version: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your iPhone, anonymous statistics can be turned off, and ads are shown by Google AdMob with the choices the law gives you.

Who is responsible

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (best height, Golden Kibble, levels, stars, skins, lives, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. If you delete the Game, this progress is deleted too. Purchases can be restored from Settings › Restore purchases.

Anonymous statistics (PostHog)

To understand how the Game is played and make it better, it sends anonymous gameplay events to PostHog, hosted on PostHog's servers in the European Union (PostHog Inc.). The events are: a run starting and ending (mode, level, height reached, kibble, taps, duration, why it ended), a level completed (stars, kibble, taps, duration), a skin unlocked, a purchase made (which product, never payment details), an ad shown (its kind), and the Game being opened or put in the background.

  • Each event carries a random identifier created by the Game on your device, plus the app version, device model, iOS version and language. There is no name, email, account or advertising identifier.
  • Like any internet request, the server sees your IP address. It may be used to derive an approximate country and is not used to identify you.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the Game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • You can turn it off at any time: Settings › Share anonymous statistics. From then on, nothing is sent.
  • Events are kept for at most 24 months.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). Rewarded videos are always your choice (continue a run, double kibble, start at 100 m, refill lives). A full-screen ad can appear between Endless runs, at most every few runs; the Pro Pup Pass removes these.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU), the Game shows Google's consent form at launch. Ads are only requested once that form allows it. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency). Before the first ad, iOS asks whether the Game may track you. Only if you allow it can Google use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • What Google may process: your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, the advertising identifier (only with your permission), device and app information, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance. Google uses it to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, with your consent, personalise them.
  • Changing your mind: Settings › Ad privacy choices in the Game (shown where the law requires it), and iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Google may process data outside the EEA under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Pro Pup Pass and the skins are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details or your Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent and think your child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and ask for portability. Because the statistics are anonymous and we have no accounts, we may be unable to link data to you; tell us what you need and we will do what we can. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium that is the Data Protection Authority. Residents of California and other US states have similar rights; we do not sell personal data.

Changes

If the Game starts handling data differently, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
